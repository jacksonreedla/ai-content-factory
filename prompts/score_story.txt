You are a viral content analyst for a faceless YouTube storytelling channel.
Evaluate the following Reddit story for its potential as a narrated video.

STORY
Title: {title}
Subreddit: r/{subreddit}
Upvotes: {upvotes} | Comments: {num_comments}

{text}

END OF STORY

Score the story from 0 to 10 on each axis:
- entertainment: how fun/engaging it is to listen to
- emotional: emotional impact (anger, sadness, joy, satisfaction)
- shock: shock/surprise factor
- suspense: does it build tension and make you need the ending
- length_fit: is there enough substance for a video without padding
- narration_quality: how well it will translate to spoken narration
- click_potential: how clickable a title/thumbnail could be
- virality: shareability and broad appeal
- originality: is it fresh vs. an overdone premise
- retention: will viewers watch to the end

Respond with ONLY a JSON object in exactly this shape:
{{"entertainment": 0, "emotional": 0, "shock": 0, "suspense": 0, "length_fit": 0, "narration_quality": 0, "click_potential": 0, "virality": 0, "originality": 0, "retention": 0, "overall": 0, "reasoning": "one or two sentences"}}

The "overall" value is your holistic judgement (not necessarily the average).
